## AerialMetric-Oblique

AerialMetric-Oblique is constructed from multiple publicly available UAV
photogrammetry datasets and resources, including:

- UrbanBIS
- GauU-SceneV2
- UAVScenes
- UrbanScene3D
- OpenDroneMap public datasets
- Esri Sample Drone Datasets
