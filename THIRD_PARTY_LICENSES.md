AerialMetric incorporates or derives data from several third-party datasets and
resources. The copyright and ownership of all third-party content remain with
their respective authors and data providers.

AerialMetric does not relicense third-party content. Users must comply with the
original licenses and terms of use of the corresponding data sources.

If any terms in this repository conflict with the original license or terms of
a third-party resource, the original terms take precedence.
